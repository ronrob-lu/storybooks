# Book 2: Realm Explorations

## The Surface Realm

The surface realm spans endless oceans, towering pine forests, and vast grasslands.
Wild flora blossoms in spring, while snow blankets northern slopes in winter.

## Subterranean Wonders

Deep beneath the bedrock lie caverns filled with luminescent crystals and underground rivers.
Traversing these depths requires preparation, light, and caution.
---
## Sky and Atmosphere

High above the clouds, gentle winds carry floating landmasses and mysterious mist.
Scholars speak of floatlands where gravity behaves strangely and air feels pristine.
