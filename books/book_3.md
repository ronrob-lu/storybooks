# Book 3: Compendium of Flora & Fauna

## Herbology Guide

1. **Golden Fern**: Found near freshwater streams. Known for its soothing properties.
2. **Shadow Moss**: Thrives in damp caverns without sunlight.
3. **Mese Shrub**: Emits a faint glow when touched by static charge.

## Wildlife Notes

- **Cave Beetles**: Harmless creatures that feed on subterranean fungi.
- **Mountain Eagles**: Soar high near cliff tops, nesting in stone ledges.
