# Book 4: Arcane Portals & Dimensions

## Portal Mechanics

Dimensional doorways link faraway places across space and time.
Constructing a sturdy frame from resonant blocks allows wormholes to stabilize.

## Ignition Rituals

Striking the frame with a focused energy source activates the portal core.
A humming portal opens, radiating color and subtle particles.
---
## Interdimensional Safety

- Never stand inside an unstable portal frame during closure.
- Keep landmark markers near exit portals to prevent getting disoriented.
- Always carry a guidebook when venturing into uncharted dimensions.
