# Book 5: The Alchemist's Handbook

## Fundamentals of Alchemy

Alchemy is the art of transforming raw essence into powerful elixirs.
By combining rare herbs and minerals, alchemists brew potions that enhance vitality and speed.

## Common Ingredients

1. **Sunbloom Petals**: Harvested at noon to maximize thermal energy.
2. **Crystal Dust**: Crushed quartz or Mese used as a magical catalyst.
3. **Deep Water**: Pure water drawn from underground aquifers.
---
## Advanced Brewing Techniques

- **Distillation**: Boil infusions slowly over low heat to concentrate efficacy.
- **Stabilization**: Add silver powder to prevent potion spoilage over long journeys.
- **Safety First**: Always wear insulated gloves when handling volatile reagents!
