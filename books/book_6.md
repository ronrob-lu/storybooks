# Book 6: Architectural Marvels

## Principles of Masonry

Building enduring structures requires balance between aesthetics and structural integrity.
Master builders utilize stone arches, flying buttresses, and solid foundations.

## Select Materials

- **Granite & Slate**: Ideal for external walls and heavy weather resistance.
- **Polished Marble**: Excellent for grand halls, temples, and ornamental pillars.
- **Tempered Glass**: Permits natural sunlight while insulating interiors.
---
## Designing Modern Libraries

Libraries should offer ambient lighting, comfortable seating, and organized shelving.
A central dome with high windows creates an uplifting atmosphere for scholars and readers.
