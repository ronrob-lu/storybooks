# Book 7: Legends of the Deep Sea

## Oceanic Mysteries

Below the surface of ocean waves lie forgotten sunken ruins and glowing coral reefs.
Navigators of old spoke of deep trenches guarded by ancient sea leviathans.

## Marine Encounters

- **Luminous Jellyfish**: Drift gracefully in oceanic depths, shedding soft blue light.
- **Tidal Pearls**: Rare gems found inside ancient giant clams.
---
## Seafaring Wisdom

Always check weather signs before embarking on long oceanic voyages.
When stormy skies gather, seek harbor or drop anchor in protected coves until calm returns.
