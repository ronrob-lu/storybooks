# Book 8: Stargazing and Celestial Paths

## Constellations of the Night

When dusk falls across the continent, night skies reveal intricate patterns of stars.
Astrologers map these constellations to guide night travelers and sea captains.

## Major Stellar Bodies

1. **The Polaris Star**: Pointing steadily north across all biomes.
2. **The Golden Dipper**: Signaling seasonal changes and optimal planting times.
---
## Celestial Navigation

By measuring star angles above the horizon, travelers can determine their exact coordinates.
Keep a clear view of the sky, a reliable compass, and this guide close at hand!
